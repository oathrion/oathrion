# Code of Conduct

Oathrion is built in the open, by people who disagree about code. We want that disagreement
to stay useful. This code applies to the issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
space of the project.

## What we expect

- **Criticize the code, not the person.** Say what is wrong and why. Assume the other person
  had a reason.
- **Be precise.** A claim comes with what supports it: a test, a log, a measurement.
- **Be welcoming.** Many people here write in their second language. Ask before you assume.
- **Accept a decision once it is made**, even one you argued against. Reopen it with new
  evidence, not with more insistence.

## What we do not accept

- harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory remarks of any kind;
- publishing someone's private information without their consent;
- sexualized language or imagery;
- deliberately disrupting a discussion.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove what does not follow this code, and close a discussion. In
serious or repeated cases, they may block someone from the project, temporarily or for good.
They explain their decision to the person concerned, unless doing so would put someone at
risk.

## Reporting

Write to **contact@oathrion.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers
only, and handled confidentially.

This code follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
